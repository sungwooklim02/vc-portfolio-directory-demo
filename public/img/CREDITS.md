# Image credits

Every image on this site is served from this site itself (no hotlinking). Photos were downloaded from
Wikimedia Commons on 2026-10-15 (standard thumbnail renditions, 1280–1920 px); the licence was re-read on each
file page at download time; then the photos were cropped, resized and re-encoded to AVIF + WebP with all
metadata (EXIF/XMP/IPTC/ICC) removed. They are generic **stock photos for atmosphere** on the detail pages of a
fictional portfolio; they do not show, and are not endorsed by, any company on this site.

CC0 and public-domain works need no attribution; the authors are listed anyway as a courtesy.
